QA Engineer (Manual & Automation)
About the Role
NielsenIQ is seeking a QA Engineer to ensure all software delivered to clients is stable, high-quality, and bug-free. You'll work within the R&D team, validating product functionality across environments, identifying and reporting defects, and improving overall release quality. If you're passionate about testing, quality ownership, and collaboration, we'd love to meet you.

Responsibilities
Manual Testing

Review and analyze system requirements, acceptance criteria, and technical documentation

Collaborate with QA Engineers, Developers, and Product Owners to define effective test strategies and test plans

Execute manual functional, regression, and exploratory testing across multiple environments

Maintain and execute test cases in Zephyr, ensuring proper traceability to user stories in Jira

Perform sanity and smoke testing post-deployment and ensure timely validation of releases

Verify API responses and integrations using tools like Postman

Identify, log, and retest defects with clear steps to reproduce, screenshots, and evidence

Participate actively in Agile ceremonies (grooming, sprint planning, daily stand-ups, retrospectives)

Automation Testing

Develop, maintain, and execute automated test scripts for web applications

Work with modern automation frameworks and tools such as Cypress and Playwright

Convert high-value manual test cases into reliable automated tests

Ensure automation covers critical user flows, regression scenarios, and cross-browser validations

Review automation results, analyze failures, and improve test stability and coverage

Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines where applicable

Continuously improve automation frameworks, test structure, and coding standards

Qualifications
3 to 10 years of proven experience in manual testing as a QA Engineer or similar role

Experience testing production-level products in an Agile environment

Master's/Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field

Solid understanding of QA methodologies, defect life cycle, and Agile frameworks

Ability to write and execute SQL queries to validate data accuracy

Strong skills in test documentation, troubleshooting, and bug reporting

Excellent attention to detail and analytical mindset

Strong communication, collaboration, and organizational skills

Good to Have:

Understanding of BDD concepts and test case design using Given–When–Then

Hands-on experience with Cypress and/or Playwright

Experience with Jira, Zephyr Scale, and Confluence for test and documentation management

Hands-on exposure to Postman, Jenkins, or other CI/CD tools

Understanding of test levels: smoke, sanity, regression, and exploratory testing

Knowledge of test data management and environment validation processes

Soft Skills:

High sense of ownership and accountability for deliverables

Ability to work under tight timelines while maintaining quality

Strong problem-solving attitude and curiosity to explore edge cases

Effective communicator with cross-functional teams across geographies
