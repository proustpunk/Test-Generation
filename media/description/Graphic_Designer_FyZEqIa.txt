Job Title: Graphic Designer

Responsibilities:
- Create visual concepts for digital and print media using tools like Adobe Creative Suite.
- Design user-friendly interfaces for websites and mobile applications.
- Collaborate with marketing teams to develop branding materials and campaigns.
- Produce motion graphics and animations as needed.
- Stay updated on design trends to deliver innovative solutions.

Requirements:
- Bachelor’s degree in Graphic Design or a related field.
- Proficiency in design tools such as Photoshop, Illustrator, and Figma.
- Strong understanding of typography, color theory, and layout design principles.
- Excellent communication skills for collaborating with stakeholders.
