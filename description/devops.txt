Job Title: DevOps Engineer

Location: [Insert Location]
Job Type: Full-Time
Department: Engineering / IT Operations

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled DevOps Engineer to join our dynamic team. The ideal candidate will have a strong understanding of cloud computing, automation, and the continuous delivery pipeline. As a DevOps Engineer, you will work closely with development and IT operations teams to automate and streamline our processes, and ensure a robust and scalable infrastructure.

Responsibilities:
Develop and maintain infrastructure automation scripts using tools like Terraform, Ansible, and CloudFormation.
Manage and monitor the cloud infrastructure (AWS, Azure, GCP).
Implement continuous integration and continuous deployment (CI/CD) pipelines to automate testing and deployment processes.
Collaborate with software engineers to understand and optimize deployment and scalability requirements.
Troubleshoot and resolve issues related to system performance, security, and infrastructure.
Build and manage containerized applications with Docker and Kubernetes.
Ensure the high availability and security of production systems.
Implement and maintain monitoring and alerting systems to ensure the health and performance of the infrastructure.
Support infrastructure provisioning, scaling, and security across multiple environments.
Requirements:
Proven experience as a DevOps Engineer or similar role in IT operations.
Strong experience with cloud platforms such as AWS, Azure, or Google Cloud.
Proficiency in scripting languages like Python, Bash, or PowerShell.
Familiarity with infrastructure automation tools (e.g., Ansible, Terraform).
Experience with CI/CD tools like Jenkins, GitLab CI, or Travis CI.
Knowledge of containerization technologies like Docker and orchestration tools like Kubernetes.
Experience with version control systems like Git.
Solid understanding of networking, firewalls, and load balancing.
Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work in a fast-paced environment.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate with cross-functional teams.
Preferred Qualifications:
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field.
Certifications in cloud technologies (AWS Certified DevOps Engineer, Microsoft Certified: Azure DevOps Engineer Expert).
Experience with monitoring tools like Prometheus, Grafana, or Datadog.
Benefits:
Competitive salary and performance-based bonuses.
Health and dental insurance.
Generous paid time off and holiday schedule.
Opportunities for career advancement and growth.
If you are passionate about DevOps and have a track record of success in building scalable, automated infrastructure, we would love to hear from you!
