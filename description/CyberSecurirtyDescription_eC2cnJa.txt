Job Title: Cybersecurity Specialist

Location: Tech City, TX

Job Type: Full-Time

Job Description:
We are seeking a skilled and proactive Cybersecurity Specialist to join our team. The ideal candidate will have hands-on experience with a wide range of security tools and technologies. The role requires a deep understanding of network security, penetration testing, incident response, and vulnerability management. You will be responsible for identifying security risks, protecting our infrastructure, and managing security incidents.

Responsibilities:
- Conduct penetration testing using Metasploit, Kali Linux, and OWASP ZAP to identify and exploit vulnerabilities in web applications and network infrastructure.
- Perform network traffic analysis with Wireshark and Nmap to detect and mitigate potential threats.
- Configure, deploy, and monitor IDS/IPS systems such as Snort and Palo Alto firewalls to protect critical systems from unauthorized access.
- Implement endpoint security using tools like CrowdStrike and Symantec Endpoint Protection to protect against malware and ransomware.
- Monitor security events using Splunk, LogRhythm, and SolarWinds to detect anomalies and respond to potential incidents.
- Work closely with the IT team to integrate security controls in both cloud and on-premise environments.
- Respond to security incidents and conduct root cause analysis using tools like EnCase and FTK Imager.
- Stay current with the latest security trends, threats, and vulnerabilities, ensuring compliance with regulatory frameworks such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI-DSS.

Required Skills and Experience:
- Proficiency with security tools such as Metasploit, Burp Suite, Kali Linux, OWASP ZAP for penetration testing.
- Hands-on experience with network traffic analyzers like Wireshark and Nmap for network reconnaissance.
- Experience with SIEM tools including Splunk, LogRhythm, and IBM QRadar.
- Strong knowledge of firewalls, VPNs, and network security protocols with hands-on experience using Cisco ASA, Palo Alto, and Fortinet.
- Familiarity with endpoint protection tools such as CrowdStrike, Symantec Endpoint Protection.
- Strong understanding of threat hunting using tools like Nagios and Splunk.
- Ability to perform incident response, forensics, and remediation using EnCase and FTK Imager.
- Experience with securing cloud-based environments, containerized applications, and managing data encryption.

Education & Certifications:
- Bachelor’s degree in Information Security, Computer Science, or related field.
- Industry certifications such as CISSP, CEH, CompTIA Security+, or Cisco CyberOps are highly preferred.

We value individuals who are proactive, detail-oriented, and passionate about security. Join our team to help protect sensitive data and ensure the integrity of our IT infrastructure.
