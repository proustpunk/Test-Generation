Job Title: Robotic Engineer

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and test robotic systems for various applications.
- Develop control algorithms using programming languages like Python or C++.
- Integrate sensors (e.g., lidar, cameras) into robotic systems for enhanced functionality.
- Collaborate with AI teams to implement machine learning models in robotics.
- Conduct system diagnostics and troubleshoot mechanical or software issues.
- Stay updated with advancements in robotics technology.

Requirements:
- Bachelor’s degree in Robotics Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field.
- Experience with robotics frameworks such as ROS (Robot Operating System).
- Proficiency in CAD tools for designing robotic components.
- Strong knowledge of sensor integration and control systems.
